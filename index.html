04

Daily rewards

The Game offers a 7-day daily reward cycle. Each calendar day you can collect one reward by watching a short rewarded video ad. Here's how it works:

  • One reward per day, based on your device's local date. Coins are added once the video has been watched to the end; closing it early gives no reward.
  • Collect on consecutive days to move from Day 1 towards the Day 7 jackpot. If you skip a day, the cycle restarts at Day 1. After Day 7, a new cycle begins the next day.
  • An internet connection is required. If a video isn't available at that moment (for example there's no ad to show), please try again a little later.
  • Changing your device's date or time to collect extra rewards isn't allowed. If the clock is moved backwards, rewards are paused until the real date catches up.

Daily rewards are a bonus, not a promise. We may change the reward amounts, the length of the cycle, or pause or end the feature at any time.
